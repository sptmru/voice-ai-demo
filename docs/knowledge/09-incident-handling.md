# Incident handling and customer communication

Fictional Relay support incident procedure. Revision 2026-09-15.

## Match an incident to the evidence

Check active incident status, affected region, carrier, start time and symptom before attributing a customer's failure. A UK incident does not explain an unrelated inbound routing error or disabled account permission. Incident records are evidence and may be incomplete. Investigating is an active state; resolved historical incidents should not be presented as ongoing causes.

## Ticket and follow-up during degradation

When symptoms align with an active incident, create a support ticket that includes customer impact, representative call IDs and UTC timestamps, completed account/trunk checks and the incident ID. Explain that the diagnosis is supported by correlated evidence and state any remaining uncertainty. Offer a callback or follow-up and record the customer's preference. In this demo, callbacks, follow-ups and escalations persist locally; no email, phone call or external ticket-system dispatch occurs.

## Resolution and status updates

Do not invent an ETA or claim the carrier has restored service without evidence. Keep resolved false while the incident remains investigating and test calls still fail. Summarize nextAction as monitoring the incident and updating the customer. When engineering reports resolution, confirm a successful new customer call before closure. Save a concise validated case summary for a future conversation, not a raw transcript dump.
