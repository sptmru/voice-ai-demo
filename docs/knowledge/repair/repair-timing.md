# Repair timing and spare parts

## How long does a repair take?

How long a repair takes is determined by three factors: completing the diagnosis, getting the customer's approval of the repair quote, and obtaining the required spare parts. These factors determine the repair turnaround time. A technician must confirm the completion date.

## Same day repair and appointment duration

We cannot guarantee same day repair. A calendar slot reserves time for diagnosis; it is not a promise that the appliance will be repaired during that slot.

## Checking a completion date or parts availability

Reference documents do not establish live parts stock or readiness. Use get_repair_status for the current job record. If it contains no confirmed completion date, say so and ask an operator. Do not promise a date or spare part that has not been confirmed.
