# Supported appliances and service areas

## Supported household appliances

Relay Workshop is a fictional demonstration service. We accept domestic washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerators. Relay Wash W100/W200, Relay Dish D100 and Relay Cool C100 are fictional model names, not manufacturer instructions.

## Home visit coverage

Home visits are available in Yerevan city only. We cannot book a home visit in another city, including Gyumri, through this demo. Ask an operator about service options outside Yerevan; do not promise coverage.

## Phone and laptop repairs

Phone and laptop repair is not offered. Relay Workshop does not repair phones, smartphones or laptops. A phone or laptop needs a specialist electronics repair service. Gas appliances and industrial equipment are also outside our service scope.
