# Dishwasher filling

## Dishwasher does not fill

For fictional Relay Dish D100, ask whether other taps have water, the door is closed and an error code is displayed. Check household water supply without disassembly. Do not disconnect hoses, open valves or electrical panels, or bypass leak protection on voice instructions. Inspection determines the cause; the assistant cannot promise valve replacement. If water is on the floor, stop use and do not touch a wet socket.
