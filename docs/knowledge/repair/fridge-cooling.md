# Refrigerator cooling

## Poor cooling

For fictional Relay Cool C100, ask which compartment is affected, whether the door closes and when cooling changed. Check the temperature setting and items blocking the door without disassembly. Do not use sharp tools to defrost, open the compressor or work on the refrigerant circuit. A description cannot confirm a refrigerant leak. For burning smell or sparks, stop using the appliance and follow electrical safety guidance.
