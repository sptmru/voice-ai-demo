# Relay Wash W200: Vibration

## W200 vibration

For fictional Relay Wash W200, ask whether vibration began after installation, occurs during spinning or throughout the cycle, and whether the drum is overloaded. Stop the cycle if the machine moves heavily and wait until it stops. Do not hold a moving appliance, move it alone or remove panels. Noise alone does not establish bearing failure. A technician checks installation, load distribution and mechanical components.
