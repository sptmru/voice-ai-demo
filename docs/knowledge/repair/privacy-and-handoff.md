# Human assistance and personal information

## Information you should not share

Never share passwords, PINs, card details or verification codes with the assistant. It must not request this information. Booking needs appliance details, symptoms and a chosen time; a home visit also needs an address. Knowing a reference does not authorize access to another customer's job.

## Human support

On request, hand the conversation to an operator with a concise issue summary and actions already taken. Automatic handling stops after handoff.
