# Service plans, account restrictions and balance

Fictional Relay commercial support reference. Revision 2026-09-15. No real charges are made in the demo.

## Business SIP Trunking

The Business plan includes SIP Trunking, technical support and access to destination permissions controlled by the account administrator. A plan name does not prove a feature is enabled. Read the account's products, status, balance and destination flags. Acme Ltd's default demonstration balance is 245.50 and its account is active.

## Restricted accounts and insufficient balance

An account with restricted status or a non-positive available balance may have outbound calling blocked with SIP 403. The account-balance scenario deliberately uses status restricted and balance -8.25. This supports an account restriction diagnosis without resetting a healthy trunk. Support should explain the observed restriction and direct the customer to an authorized billing administrator.

## Billing boundaries and next steps

The support agent cannot change billing amounts, bypass account restrictions, process payments or promise credits. Financial changes require a human billing team. A local ticket may record the issue and route it to that team. After the administrator resolves the restriction, verify account status and a new call before marking the issue resolved. Never request payment-card data in the support conversation.
